Instagram / Facebook caption
2 ways to use up spring onions before they go to waste

If you are wondering what to do with too many spring onions, I cook them briefly in stir-fries, omelettes or noodle dishes. You can also chop and freeze them in bags, or pickle them in a light vinegar brine to last one to two months in the fridge. Best of all, put the roots in a glass of water on a windowsill to regrow them.

Check your local frost dates.
