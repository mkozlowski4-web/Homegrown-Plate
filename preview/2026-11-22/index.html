How to grow rhubarb homegrownplate.co.uk/plants/rhubarb/
Instagram / Facebook caption
Nobody tells you November is still rhubarb planting time

If you want to know how to grow rhubarb UK gardens thrive on, plant dormant crowns from November to March. Always pull and twist the stems at the base instead of cutting them, so you do not leave stumps that rot. Trim off the poisonous leaves and stew the stalks with sugar and orange juice for 10 minutes so nothing goes to waste.

Check your local frost dates.
